bindings are avoided.- Use is the single contextual interaction action.DODA CONTROL ARCHITECTURE — WEAPON PRESENTATION- First-person weapons use Paper 2.5D layered sprites.- Runtime transforms firing trace, projectile direction, and use trace  share one authoritative intended aim direction.- Weapon hand changes use rapid lower/raise transitions with a  hysteresis band around screen center.- Full runtime 3D weapon models are not part of the initial plan.Native GZDoom-side module:- Reads keyboard, mouse, controller, gyro, and HID data- Resolves bindings, chords, and
